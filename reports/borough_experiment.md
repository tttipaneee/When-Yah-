Generated by `python -m ml.experiment_borough` on 2026-10-10.

Event features are off here (venue zone IDs never match borough codes).

## Reading (plain)
- Area-wide prices ARE somewhat predictable: with_level forecast error is -3.8% vs
  persistence, and it ranks the rare 10% drops well (AUC above).
- But area-wide prices rarely move much (0.8% of windows drop ≥ 10% in 30 min), so following the
  advice saves +0.27% on average — not clearly above the hour-of-day rule (+0.38%).
- The rule passed on AUC only, from a handful of drop events; treat it as a hint, not proof.
